Frequently asked questions

How do I accept a check by phone?

Collect the customer's checking account and routing number over the phone, enter the payment details into the SimpleChex dashboard, and the eCheck is processed through the ACH network — no paper check required.

What is an eCheck?

An eCheck is an electronic version of a paper check. Funds move from the payer's checking account to your business account through the Automated Clearing House (ACH) network.

Is SimpleChex secure?

Yes. SimpleChex processes payments over encrypted connections and follows industry-standard security and compliance practices to protect sensitive banking information.

How long do eCheck payments take to clear?

eCheck and ACH payments typically settle within three to five business days, depending on your processing configuration.
